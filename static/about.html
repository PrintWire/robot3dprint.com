About Robot 3D Print

Covering additive manufacturing for cobot and actuator additive manufacturing — from concept to production hardware.

OUR STORY

Actuation at the Frontier of Fabrication

Robot 3D Print covers additive manufacturing for the moving joint — from strain-wave and cycloidal reducers to integrated actuators, compliant joints, end-effectors, and tool changers.

Our editorial team includes robotics and mechatronics engineers with real project experience across collaborative arms, actuation, and end-of-arm tooling.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover cobot AM from the flexure spline to the full actuator with equal depth — the drive, the joint, and the material behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for additive manufacturing in actuation — connecting builders to the suppliers reshaping how cobots move.

WHAT DRIVES US

What Sets Us Apart
